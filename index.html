How calculations work

Self-Employment Tax (15.3%): Applied to 92.35% of your net self-employment income. This covers Social Security (12.4%) and Medicare (2.9%). Half of this tax is deductible from your income.

Federal Income Tax: Calculated using marginal tax brackets based on your projected annual income including this payment, minus standard deduction and business deductions.

State Income Tax: Calculated using your state's tax rates (flat or progressive brackets).

Quarantine Amount: Total tax + 5% safety buffer to ensure you never under-save.

⚠️ This tool provides estimates only. Consult a tax professional for your specific situation.
